### 🌐 About ITG

**Info Tech Corporation of Goa Limited (ITG)** is a Government of Goa undertaking dedicated to accelerating information technology adoption, building robust digital public infrastructure, and delivering innovative e-Governance services across the state.

- 🏛️ **Digital Governance:** Architecting citizen-centric portals, digital registries, and departmental workflows.
- 🚀 **IT Infrastructure:** Facilitating state data centers, connectivity backbones, and public utility tech systems.
- 💡 **Innovation & Incubation:** Supporting regional technology growth, digital literacy, and modern web initiatives.
